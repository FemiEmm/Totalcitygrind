## Notes for the new housing plan

- Four-room rows, miniflats and big houses are not current gameplay classifications. They will need unit records and an explicit parking space for each resident.
- Starting Residential currently uses 117 single-house sprite entries and 16 double-house sprite entries. These are visual counts, not confirmed dwelling counts.
- Wealthy Residential declares 22 compound blocks, but only 17 survive the final map reservations. Count final map entries rather than the original block list.
- Coast City removes four original residence entries to place the petrol station and mechanic: upper-home-4-26, upper-home-4-28, lower-home-28-36 and lower-home-28-38. These are excluded above.
- The separate Atlantic Waterfront district declares no additional buildings; coastal-column houses already belong to the upper/lower layouts and are not counted twice.
- Offices, shops, hotels and nightlife buildings are excluded; there is no residential-unit definition for them.
